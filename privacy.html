Privacy

Short version: Vón doesn't collect anything. Your data stays on your phone.

What Vón stores

Your countdowns, dates, places, to-dos and notes are saved on your device only. A small copy of your upcoming countdowns is shared with Vón's own widgets through an app group on the same device, so the widgets can show them.

What Vón doesn't do

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No servers: the app doesn't send your data anywhere.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone. They don't go through any server.

Importing a plan

If you paste a plan made with an AI assistant, Vón reads the text on your device. Vón doesn't contact that assistant; what you share with it is covered by that service's own policy.

Links

Sources shown on to-dos open in Safari. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Backups and deleting data

Your data can be part of your own iCloud or computer backup, as with any app. Deleting a countdown removes it; deleting the app removes everything.
