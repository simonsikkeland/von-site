Vón

Countdowns for trips, events and goals, with the to-dos that come before them.

Support

Questions, bugs or a country you'd like a theme for? Email von.app@icloud.com. I usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

The widget shows no countdowns. Open Vón once after installing or updating it, then edit the widget and choose a countdown.

I don't get reminders. Check Settings › Notifications › Vón. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; iOS keeps up to 64 at a time, so the nearest ones come first.

Are the deadlines guaranteed? No. Themes are checked against official sources (shown on each to-do) and dated, but rules change. Always confirm with the source before you rely on it.

Where is my data? Only on your phone. See the privacy policy.
